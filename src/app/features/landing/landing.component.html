The DataSpace Portal

Standardized. Secure. Real-time.

A secure data-sharing platform connecting partners, organisations, and systems in a trusted ecosystem for live, standardized data exchange — built on open dataspace standards.

Why DataSpace Portal

A purpose-built platform to share live, standardized data securely across your network — with the highest standards of compliance and trust.

End-to-End Security

Sovereign data exchange with end-to-end encryption, verifiable credentials, and granular access policies — your data, your rules.

Standards-Based Compliance

Full alignment with European data-protection legislation, open dataspace specifications, and international best practices.

High Availability

Cloud-native infrastructure with 24/7 availability, ensuring uninterrupted access to live data for time-sensitive operations.

Multi-Dataspace

Participate in multiple dataspaces simultaneously — different projects, supply chains, and partner networks — through a single identity.

Real-Time Insights

Dashboards and audit trails to monitor data flows, track transfer agreements, and maintain full transparency across partnerships.

Open Standards

Built on Eclipse Dataspace Components (EDC) and IDS/GAIA-X standards, ensuring interoperability with the broader European data ecosystem.
